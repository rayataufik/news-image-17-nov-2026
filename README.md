A one-image summary of the world's news on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, made for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/?ref=vnhxm4py) bounty "Sum up 17 November's world news in one image".

The image is a dense newspaper front page: the date in full across the masthead, 13 illustrated lead stories in three rows, a world map with the stories pinned, a 30-item "Also on Tuesday" list, and a "By the numbers" strip. 43 stories across 6 continents, all from the one day. U.S. strikes on Iran after the Hormuz tanker attacks, Ukraine's hit on the Kerch Bridge, the Quad summit in Manila, the outage cascade across banks, flights, Apple and the NSA, NVIDIA's board refusing Jensen Huang's return, and the U.S. losing its measles elimination status — plus Ebola in DR Congo, COP31, Gaza, and 30 more. Every story is as reported by Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera, WHO and the UN on the day, and every drawing is original.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the front page (1024×1024, under 1 MiB).
- `design.html` — the page's source: one HTML file with inline SVG drawings, rendered to `entry.png` with headless Chrome.
- `main.py` — the entry program: copies `entry.png` to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
